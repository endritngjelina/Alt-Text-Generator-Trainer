Alt Text Best Practices

🎯

Be Specific & Accurate

  • Describe what's actually in the image
  • Include relevant details about colors, objects, and actions
  • Avoid subjective interpretations
✂️

Keep It Concise

  • Aim for 125 characters or less
  • Focus on the most important elements
  • Avoid redundant phrases like "image of" or "picture of"
🎨

Context Matters

  • Consider where the image appears
  • Match the description to user needs
  • Include information relevant to the content
⚡

Functional Images

  • For buttons/links: describe the action
  • For icons: explain their purpose
  • For decorative images: use empty alt=""

📖 Quick Examples

❌
Poor:
"Image of a dog"
✅
Better:
"Golden retriever puppy playing with a red ball on grass"
❌
Poor:
"A very beautiful sunset over the ocean with amazing colors in the sky and waves crashing on the beach"
✅
Better:
"Sunset over ocean with orange and pink sky"

Advanced Guidelines

Complex Images

For charts, graphs, and infographics, provide a brief summary and link to detailed data.

Text in Images

Always include any text that appears in the image in your alt description.

Emotional Context

When relevant, include emotional context (e.g., "person smiling" vs "person frowning").

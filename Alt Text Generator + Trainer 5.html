📊 Quality Analysis

💡 Best Practices for Alt Text:

  • Be specific and concise: Aim for 125 characters or less when possible
  • Describe the content and function: What is the image showing and why is it there?
  • Avoid redundancy: Don't start with "Image of..." or "Picture of..."
  • Include relevant context: Describe what's important for understanding the content
  • Consider the context: Alt text should fit the purpose of the image on the page
  • Skip decorative images: Use empty alt="" for purely decorative images
